Mod rules

When every comma-separated item in "If rolled" has been rolled on any wheel, the mods in "Add mods" join your run. Use | for "or", so Bard | Healer, Calamity Mod means (Bard or Healer) and Calamity Mod. Names must match an option exactly (capitals don't matter). Mods added by one rule count as rolled for the others. Tick "Always required" for mods a class can't run without; the cancel list leaves those in place and only waives the rest as compat mods.

    Difficulty wheels

    These wheels spin in the difficulty window, which opens after Spin all. A wheel with no mod set is a standard difficulty and follows the "Difficulty roll" switch. A wheel with a mod set only appears when that mod is in the run, whether it was rolled or added by a rule, and follows the "Mod difficulty roll" switch. List several mods with commas to need all of them. A wheel can name other wheels in "Replaces" to take their place while it appears.

      Rolling a mod difficulty listed under "Set the top standard difficulty" (Malice) sets the standard difficulty to its top option. Masochist is not listed, so Master with Masochist is possible. Rolling the top standard difficulty pushes the mod difficulties listed under "Pushed to the top" to their own top option, so Death becomes Malice and Eternity becomes Masochist, while Revengeance stays as rolled. Malice is only possible on the top standard difficulty. On a combined wheel (one that needs several mods, such as Calamity and Fargo together) the top standard difficulty always gives the options listed under "Always set on combined wheels", even when the roll was Normal, because Masochist overrides Death and Malice. Forced results are marked "set by" in the window and in Your run.
